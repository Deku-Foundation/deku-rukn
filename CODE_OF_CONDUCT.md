# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, language, or disability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome Arabic-language contributions and help make technical discussion accessible.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where practical, and may edit or remove harmful content or restrict participation. This policy is not a substitute for GitHub's Terms of Service.
